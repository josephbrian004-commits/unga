Cook it right

Three kitchen staples, start to finish.

Ugali
  1. Boil 2½ cups of water in a heavy pot.
  2. Stir in a little unga to make a smooth paste, then simmer for two minutes.
  3. Add the rest gradually, pressing out lumps with a wooden spoon.
  4. Cover on low heat for 5 minutes, then stir firmly until it pulls from the sides.
Chapati
  1. Mix 3 cups wheat flour with ½ teaspoon salt and a spoon of oil.
  2. Add warm water slowly and knead for 8 minutes until soft.
  3. Rest 30 minutes, roll into balls, then flat circles brushed with oil.
  4. Fold, roll again, and cook on a hot pan until golden on both sides.
Uji (porridge)
  1. Mix 4 tablespoons porridge flour with a little cold water until smooth.
  2. Pour into 2 cups of boiling water, stirring all the time.
  3. Simmer 10 minutes until thick, then add milk or sugar to taste.
